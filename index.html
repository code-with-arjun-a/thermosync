Cooling a room harder costs energy, so expect the adaptive controller to trade extra energy for a cooler, steadier room when it is busy. The rise is simulated with a simple lumped model: air mass ×4 for furniture, a fixed envelope gain from outdoors, and a proportional AC.

Model

Q_internal = N_h·100 W + N_c·180 W ΔT = Δt·Q_internal / (ρ·C_p·V) Δt = 300 s ρ·C_p ≈ 1210 J/(m³·°C) T_target = max(T_min, T_base − (24.8·N_h + 44.6·N_c) / V) T_min = 18 °C Outdoor term (toggle): T_target = max(T_min, T_base − [24.8·N_h + 44.6·N_c + 0.248·U·max(0, T_out − T_base)] / V) U = 150 W/°C
